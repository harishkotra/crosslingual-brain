# Bundled sample documents

These two PDFs are real documents, included so the demo works on first visit without a signup.

1. `health_policy.pdf` — Arogya Sanjeevani Policy wording, HDFC ERGO General Insurance.
   Publicly distributed policy document, downloaded from the insurer's customer portal on 2026-10-18.
   Used here as a stand-in for the insurance documents people actually keep.

2. `discharge_summary.pdf` — "Good Discharge Summary Sample, Medicine", University Health Network
   (uhnmodules.ca). A publicly published teaching sample of a hospital discharge summary.

Neither file is modified. Embeddings are computed locally in the browser; the files never leave the
device. Replace them with your own documents by dropping PDFs into the app, or delete `public/corpus/`
before publishing if you would rather ship without them.
